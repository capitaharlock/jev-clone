# Training status · 2026-10-15

TinyJev is training continuously on the local MLX loop. The current champion
still comes from cycle 5; cycle 31 is running. Later candidates have not passed
the promotion gate.

## Measured results

- Promotion battery: 95.53% mean across the written order and eight shuffles.
  Written-order accuracy is 382/400 (95.5%); the worst order is 94.75%.
- Benchmark v2 refined: 9,828/10,000 (98.28%). This benchmark is diagnostic
  and saturated, so it does not select the champion.
- JEv reached 400/400 and Qwen reached 98.25% on the old 400-row battery. Those
  figures remain visible as historical references, but that comparison is on
  hold until every model is measured on benchmark v3.
- Benchmark v3 alpha is ready with 18,710 development cases, 6,316 sealed
  cases and a 1,350-case probe across 9 arenas and 87 domains. The first
  champion score has not finished yet.

## Data expansion

The independent dataset factory now has a 35-source catalogue mapped to all 51
branches of its first world taxonomy. Ten sources and 6.43 GB are on disk.
The first compact pass contains 134,802 normalized text records in 25.5 MB of
gzip JSONL.

The v3 builders have produced 153,750 real training decisions from products,
code, science and Stack Exchange. They remain outside the training mix until
licence, deduplication and benchmark-overlap checks pass.

## Current direction

Short cycles remain active as a regression control. The main route to the next
gain is broader real data followed by a long run, with v3 measured at each
checkpoint. JEv is an evaluator only until TypeSafe's terms permit training on
its output.
